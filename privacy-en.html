Privacy Policy — Photo Settings Guide

Last updated: 30 September 2026

The short version

There is no app account, advertising, or analytics. Reading, profiles, calculators and the built-in assistant work locally. Optional location access and notifications are controlled by your choices. Weather, place lookup and Apple purchase services are described below; the app is not entirely network-free.

What stays on your device

Your chosen language, sensor format and camera brand, saved profiles, reading progress and cached weather forecast are stored locally on your iPhone. This describes the app's local storage, not the retention practices of the external services below or any device backups managed outside the app.

Features that use external services

Weather: refreshing the forecast in Today sends the selected coordinates and forecast options to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com). Its terms are at https://open-meteo.com/en/terms.

Place lookup: searching by name uses Apple's geocoding service with the entered place text. Looking up a place name from a location uses the coordinates.

Purchases: the app uses Apple's StoreKit services to load product information, make purchases and restore purchases. Product loading can occur without opening Today. These features may require internet access even though the book, profiles, calculators and assistant work offline.

This policy describes the app's service calls. It does not make claims about the providers' retention practices or all metadata handled by their services.

Location

If you allow it and request your location, the app requests a location fix for local calculations, place naming and the forecast. The last location and cached forecast are stored locally; updating the forecast uses the weather service and obtaining a place name uses geocoding as described above. The app does not maintain a movement history.

You can refuse location access and choose a place manually. Searching for a place by name may require internet access. Local reading, profiles, calculators and the assistant do not require location permission.

Notifications

Shooting reminders are optional local notifications. You can enable or disable them in the app; notification permission is also controlled in iOS Settings. Enabling reminders does not make the assistant a network service.

Photographs

The photographs in the app ship with it. It does not read your photo library, your camera roll or your files.

Children

The app is not specifically designed for children and does not require an account. Optional network features may send the data described above; we do not claim that the service providers process no data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and the date above will change.
